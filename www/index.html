Explore Alor Setar Kedah, Malaysia Welcome to Alor Setar

Alor Setar is the capital city of Kedah, affectionately known as the "Rice Bowl of Malaysia" for its sweeping green and gold paddy fields. Blending rich historical heritage with modern landmarks, it is a tranquil yet culturally vibrant getaway.

The Skyline

The city is anchored by the Alor Setar Tower (Menara Alor Setar). Standing 165.5 metres tall, this telecommunications tower offers panoramic views of the city and the surrounding plains.

Historical Heart

A short distance away is Zahir Mosque, one of Malaysia's oldest and most beautiful mosques, topped with five black domes that symbolise the Five Pillars of Islam.
